What you pay, and to whom

Held in escrow by a Maltese lawyerWhen you order, you pay the full car price, landed in Malta into the client account of an independent advocate in Malta. It covers the car, shipping and insurance, import duty and VAT. The lawyer releases the money to the seller, Hosokawa Pte. Ltd., in one payment, only after you have received the car. If the car is not shipped on time, or cannot be registered in Malta because of a problem with the car, you get a refund.
Paid by you in MaltaThe registration costs shown on each car: registration tax, plates and local fees, paid directly to the Maltese authorities and providers. These are estimates and may change.
