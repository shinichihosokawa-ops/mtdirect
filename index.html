What you pay, and to whom

Held in escrow by a Maltese lawyerWhen you order, you pay the full Car price, landed in Malta into the client account of an independent advocate in Malta. It covers the car, shipping and insurance, import duty and VAT. The lawyer releases the money to Douou Jidosha (Japan) only after the car has been shipped and the bill of lading is issued. If the car is not shipped on time or fails the SVA inspection, you get a full refund.
Paid by you in MaltaThe Registration costs shown below each car: registration tax, SVA inspection, plates and local fees. You pay these directly to the Maltese authorities and providers. The amounts are estimates and may change.
