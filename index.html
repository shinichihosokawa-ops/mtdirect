What you pay, and to whom

Held in escrow by a Maltese lawyerWhen you order, you pay the price delivered to Malta port into the client account of an independent advocate in Malta. It covers the car, transport to Nagoya, the SVA inspection, the JEVIC mileage certificate, sea freight, insurance and the escrow fee. The lawyer releases the money to the seller, Hosokawa Pte. Ltd., in one payment, only after you have received the car. If the car is not shipped on time, or cannot be registered in Malta because of a problem with the car, you get a refund.
Paid by you in MaltaAs the importer, you pay import VAT (18%), import duty (0% for Japanese-made cars under the EU–Japan agreement), port handling and customs clearance, the registration tax, and registration fees, plates, the VRT test and the first road licence. VAT is charged on the price you paid for the car plus port costs. The amounts on each car are estimates; Transport Malta sets the registration tax.

Cars marked “In stock at Douou” are in stock at Douou Jidosha in Hokkaido (photos by Douou Jidosha, November 2026). The price delivered to Malta port is what you pay into escrow. Import VAT, registration tax and local fees are estimates paid by you in Malta; the registration tax is set by Transport Malta. Cars ship from Nagoya after the SVA inspection in Japan. Prices assume €1 = ¥177.
